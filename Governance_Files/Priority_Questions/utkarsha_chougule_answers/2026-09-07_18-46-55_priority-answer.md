# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Does the address-correction flow write back to production owner records, and who approves that change?

Answer:
The address correction is submitted as a correction request rather than directly modifying the production owner record. The original county appraisal-derived owner data remains unchanged until the correction is reviewed and approved through the appropriate process. The correction should maintain an audit trail containing the submitted change, requester, timestamp, and approval/rejection details.
