# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Do our Coverage owner-roll pages publish individual mineral-owner names, and is that cleared?

Answer:
Two separate things need separating here: the current live website, and the upgrade.

On the current live website — yes, owner names are already published. This is the existing, inherited state, not something introduced by the upgrade work. Verified live this morning on the Karnes County page, logged out:

Owner names in full, 20 per page, unmasked and visible without an account
Interest type per owner (Royalty, Overriding Royalty, Working Interest)
County counts: 16,080 total mineral owners, 2,502 total leases
Everything of commercial value is already masked with *** for logged-out visitors — lease counts per owner, ownership category, all appraised values, average appraised per owner, county ownership share, and the full Top Owners table. The page carries a "Data Locked — Login or Create a Free Account" notice.

So the live treatment is already conservative in its shape: the name is the discovery hook, the value is gated.

On the upgrade — nothing is live. Nothing has been built or deployed. No new page, no new URL, no change to how names are handled. What exists today is planning material: the architecture blueprint, the migration care and safeguards plan, the current-assets baseline, the Master Spec skeleton. We are deliberately producing as much supporting documentation as possible and submitting all of it to the governance workbench so the decision can be made on evidence rather than assumption.

The final decision is Ryan's. These are the facts he'd want in front of him:

The data is public record. Texas county appraisal districts appraise each owner's producing mineral interest under Property Tax Code §23.175, and those rolls are public. Republishing public record is lawful, and it is standard in this market — MineralHolders publishes individuals by name at scale.
Most published names are entities, but not all. The same visible Karnes list includes "2012 Jennifer Martin, Trust", "2011 Charles W Combs Family", "2015 Janysek Family Mineral, Trust" and "210 Reiffert Land, Trust" — individuals and families holding through trusts. Worth knowing precisely, so the decision is made with the real picture rather than an assumption that it's all corporate.
The proposed architecture keeps the line where it already is — names appear in the county listing; individual owner detail pages stay gated, noindex, on opaque IDs. No indexed page per person.
What we're asking for is a confirmation of the existing position, not approval of a change. If Ryan wants it treated differently in the new build, now is the point where it costs nothing to change — the structure isn't built yet. That is exactly why it's being raised at the documentation stage rather than after development starts.
