# Priority Question Answer

Question:
What is the abort or rollback trigger if the accepted 2-month traffic loss runs longer or deeper?

Answer:
There is no abort or rollback trigger, deliberately. And it's worth saying plainly up front: nothing is lost yet, because nothing has been changed yet. What exists today is planning work — the architecture plan, the SEO migration care and safeguards document, the current-assets baseline — all being submitted to the governance workbench before anything is executed.

The exposure is bounded by construction, which is what makes no-rollback a reasonable posture.

Roughly 80% of our organic traffic comes from blogs, glossary and marketing pages, and none of them are touched. On the measured baseline it's higher still — blogs, glossary and marketing together are 85.9% of all clicks from 20.4% of pages, and they sit on the explicit protect list for the migration.

The entire section under discussion is 14.1% of clicks. So even in the worst imaginable case — the whole de-indexed section going to zero and staying there — the maximum exposure is that 14%, against a national architecture that is the point of the whole exercise. A ~20% dip for a couple of months is defensible when the return is an architecture that scales to every state instead of one. Mineral View gets a structure where adding New Mexico or Oklahoma is a data load, not another rebuild.
