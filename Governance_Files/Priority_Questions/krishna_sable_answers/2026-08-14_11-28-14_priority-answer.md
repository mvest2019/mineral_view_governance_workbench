# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Does the planned master sheet replace the existing monthly organic-traffic report, or is it a second parallel analytics source?

Answer:
Neither replaces the other, and it must not become a parallel source of truth.

Monthly organic-traffic report — measures what happened on our site. Source: Search Console. Answers "how are we performing?"
Master sheet — decides what to build next. Sources: Ahrefs for market demand, Search Console for our position. Answers "which pages are justified?"

Different questions, different cadence, different audiences. Search Console remains the single source of truth for our own traffic numbers — the master sheet borrows from it rather than competing with it. Where Ahrefs and Search Console disagree about our own performance, Search Console wins; Ahrefs understates us by roughly 30%.
