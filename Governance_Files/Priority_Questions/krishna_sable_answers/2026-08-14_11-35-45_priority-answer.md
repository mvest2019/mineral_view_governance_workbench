# Priority Question Answer

Question:
How exactly are the AI assistant citations measured, and who verified the "we are the most-cited site" claim before it is used anywhere externally?

Answer:
The measurement. A single Ahrefs endpoint, site-explorer-ai-responses-count, queried per domain for the United States. For each AI platform it returns two numbers: how many times that domain was cited in AI answers, and how many distinct pages of it were cited. Platforms covered: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode. Pulled 2026-08-13 for seven domains on identical parameters.

What the claim actually is. Mineral View recorded 62 citations, the highest of the seven sites measured — ahead of shalexp (48) and pheasantenergy (45).

Precisely stated, it means: most-cited among these seven domains, per Ahrefs, on one pull date, in the US. It does not mean most-cited in the industry, or most-cited overall. Those are different claims and the data doesn't support them.
