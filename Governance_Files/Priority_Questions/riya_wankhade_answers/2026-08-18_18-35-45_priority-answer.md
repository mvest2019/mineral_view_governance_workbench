# Priority Question Answer

Question:
What are the documented pass/fail criteria for a daily validation cycle, and who signs off that it passed?

Answer:
A daily validation cycle is considered successful when scrapers complete as expected, record counts are within the expected range, and no critical missing, duplicate, or inconsistent data is identified. Any exceptions are investigated and documented. The responsible technical/project team reviews the results and provides the final sign-off.
