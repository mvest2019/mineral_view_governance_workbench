# Priority Question Answer

Question:
What documented pass/fail criteria define a day's scraped data as "validated"?

Answer:
A day's data is considered validated when the scraper completes successfully, expected records are present, required fields are populated, and no significant duplicates, missing data, or inconsistencies are found. Any exceptions are reviewed, documented, and resolved or escalated before final validation.
