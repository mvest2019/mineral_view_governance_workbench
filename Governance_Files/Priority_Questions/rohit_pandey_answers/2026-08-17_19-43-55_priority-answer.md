# Priority Question Answer

Question:
What is the minimum audit score required to publish, and were the 85/100 content-quality gaps closed before the page went live?

Answer:
The minimum publish score is 90 — deliberately high and explicit in the rubric ("Publishing threshold: 90… fifteen pages scoring 95 will build more operator authority than sixty scoring 70"), with 75–89 defined as "revise before publishing — identify the lowest-scoring dimension and fix it" (usually Dimension 4 actionability or Dimension 2 Texas/RRC data depth), and either fatal flaw (a wrong governed technical claim, or zero information gain over the SERP incumbents) blocking publication regardless of total. So an 85/100 page is not cleared to go live as-is; on the record available in this environment there is no re-audit showing the gaps were closed — no post-revision score for that page exists in the files or session logs. The required action is a targeted revision of its weakest dimension and a re-score to ≥90 before (or, if it's already live, retroactively validating) publication; if it shipped at 85, that's a process breach to correct, not a threshold to reinterpret.
