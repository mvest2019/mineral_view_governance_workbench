Where is the internal daily tracking sheet stored, and who has access, given it holds registrations, lease claims, and Contact Us inquiries?

Answer:
The daily tracking sheet is a Google Sheet in the team's Drive workspace, alongside the other operational folders (MineralView_Operations, Needs Attention — Ownership Verification), and access is limited by Drive sharing permissions to the internal MineralView team — the same regime governing all PII artifacts in this project: internal-only, never in git, never link-shared externally. No formal access register exists in the project record — reading the sheet's Drive sharing panel and recording it is the one-step action if the workbench needs a named list.
