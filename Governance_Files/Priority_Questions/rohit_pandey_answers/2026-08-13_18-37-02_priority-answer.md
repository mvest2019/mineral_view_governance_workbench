# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Who owns title verification for entity, institutional, and foundation claims, and what evidence closes those cases?

Answer:
The dossier pipeline only flags and holds — it never verifies title (standing rule: "never assert ownership/title"; the product itself states "we don't verify title or heirship — a records/attorney question"). Resolution is owned by the ops/customer-success workflow via the Needs Attention — Ownership Verification Google Drive register (index + one-pager per held account, the mechanism created with Krishna for exactly these claim-vs-title risks), working with the member. Evidence that closes a case: proof of the member's authority over the entity — officer/partner/trustee/registered-agent documentation (e.g., Texas SoS filing, trust or LP/Inc paperwork) plus the portal's relationship capture — with the 2025 CAD roll confirming the entity actually holds the claimed leases. Absent that proof the claim stays withheld (as with the Fasken over-claims); for the ISD Education Foundation case the bar is authorized-representative proof, otherwise the claim is removed as invalid.
