Question:
Can the 4 provisional dossiers be shared with members before ownership and title verification, and who approves that?

Answer:
No — held dossiers cannot be shared with members before ownership/title verification closes; that's the standing policy, not a pending decision. These 4 are held precisely because the recorded owner is an entity, not the member — sharing a report that prices an entity's holdings (up to ~$22.5M) to an individual whose connection is unproven would assert exactly what the pipeline is forbidden to assert ("never assert ownership/title"). They ship only after the member proves authority over the entity (officer/trustee/registered-agent evidence via the Needs Attention — Ownership Verification process), which reclassifies them to a shippable bucket. Approval authority sits with the dossier workstream owner (marketing/ops) through the per-dossier release review — and note the contrast: only the ⚠️ usable-with-caveat class may ship pre-verification, because its unverified-title framing is baked into the text; the 🔍 HOLD class never does.
