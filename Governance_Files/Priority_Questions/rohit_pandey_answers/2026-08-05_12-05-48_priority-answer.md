# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Who reviews the namesake-flagged cases, and what stops a wrong name match from reaching a user?

Answer:
Namesake-flagged cases are currently under active development and do not yet have a finalized review workflow. When multiple owners share the same name and there is no claimed lease to verify identity, the pipeline does not assign ownership and instead flags the case as ambiguous to avoid presenting incorrect information.

To improve this, the next planned approach is to generate multiple dossier options for non-claim users where appropriate, allowing further verification before any ownership information is associated with a specific user.
