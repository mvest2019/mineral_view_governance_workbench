Question:
Who owns the decision that the new community design has no per-question indexable URLs, and must engineering restore them before launch?

Answer:
URL structure and indexability decisions belong to Gautami under D-001 (the spec puts "redirects, canonicals, indexability and sitemap membership" in her veto scope) — engineering/Krishna's rebuilt structure implements the ruling, it doesn't make it; no recorded D-001 decision authorizes dropping per-question URLs. And yes, per-question indexable URLs must exist at launch, because the approved migration plan is built on them: the 132 KEEP threads retain their exact URLs carrying 95% of community search clicks (647 of 678 WW) and 96% of impressions, and the 129 MERGE/DROP redirects need thread-level targets — without per-question URLs those rankings are forfeited and community (4.8% of site organic) has no landing surface. Note the nuance: the threads don't need to be in the sitemap (G9 lists only the 12 category indexes), but they must remain live, crawlable, one-URL-per-question — so the requirement is "restore before launch, or take a documented D-001 ruling from Gautami accepting the traffic loss."
