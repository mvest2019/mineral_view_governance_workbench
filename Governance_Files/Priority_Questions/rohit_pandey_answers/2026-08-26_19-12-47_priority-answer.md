Question:
Does the payout calculator state its assumptions and carry the educational-only / no-advice disclaimer?

Answer:
Assumptions: yes — the calculator carries a proper assumptions statement right at the tool ("This calculator provides a simplified planning estimate only. Actual payout is determined by the costs, revenue, and rules defined in the governing agreement. Production decline, commodity prices, operating expenses, and allowed deductions will affect the real payout date"), which correctly frames its outputs (flat estimate, 5%-decline estimate, BPO→APO working-interest change). Disclaimer: partial — the "planning estimate only" line gives the educational framing at the tool, and the page-end disclaimer covers "not legal or accounting advice," but neither says "not financial/investment advice" — a real gap on this specific page, since payout timing is directly an investment-economics output aimed at working-interest audiences. Fix is the same one-liner as on the LOE calculator: add "not financial, investment, or accounting advice" to the calculator's estimate note (Rohit's HTML, dev deploy), and codify the block in the Glossary HTML Rules so every future tool inherits it.
