# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Are the district-code and numeric owner-number breakages caused by the database refresh itself, and who owns preventing recurrence?

Answer:
Yes — both breakages were caused by the reload itself, not by consumer code: the earlier 2025 load used the correctly zero-padded districtcode and the reload dropped the leading zeros (a documented regression), and the same load changed ownernumber to numeric — pure ETL format drift. Nikhil and the DB/ETL team own the fix and the recurrence prevention — it was escalated as a formal blocker (2026-07-27, "2025 CAD roll — BLOCKER for Nikhil") requesting the re-pad plus cleanups (junk district codes 19/29/39/49/59, 1,889 null district+county rows) and the missing {districtcode, leasenumber} compound index. Prevention means format/schema validation inside the staged county-load process (padded 2-char codes, string types, index present) before data is published, since every join-based consumer including the live portal would hit the same zero-match failure at cutover. The dossier tool independently hardened itself (id_variants, sstr()) but that's defense, not the fix.
