# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Who has authority to release a dossier, and which of the five review classifications are cleared to ship?

Answer:
Release authority sits with the dossier workstream owner (marketing/ops) — every dossier is individually reviewed and released one at a time per the standing quality rule; no dossier ships automatically, and problem cases escalate via the Needs Attention — Ownership Verification register rather than to a separate approver. Of the five classifications: ✅ verified-final (6) is cleared to ship now; 🔗 confirm-relationship (14) ships only after the member's relationship to the recorded owner is captured (Self/Spouse/Heir/Trustee etc.); ⚠️ ownership-caveat (14) may ship with the unverified-title caveat rendered (the writer rules mandate "claimed, not verified" framing). The two HOLD buckets never ship: title-verification (5 entity/institutional/foundation cases) until authority evidence closes them, and over-claim withheld (3) which are do-not-use outright — likewise the conflicting-claim and internal accounts.
