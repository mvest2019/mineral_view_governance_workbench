Question:
Do the dossiers show a CAD value or MVEstimate figure, and do they carry the estimate / no-investment-advice disclaimer?

Answer:
Both figures appear, deliberately separated and labeled: the ownership section shows the CAD appraised value from the county appraisal roll (Mineral_Owners_Data_2025) as a recorded fact, while asset value, projected royalty income, and reserve life come from the MVestimate model and are explicitly framed as estimates. Yes, the disclaimers are carried in the generated file itself, verified verbatim: "Your share = whole-lease estimate × your total owner interest… Estimate, not an appraisal or offer," "Estimate, not a guarantee," plus the writer hard rule baked into every header — "always say 'estimate,' never 'appraisal' or 'offer'" — and a sources section repeating "Estimate — not an appraisal or offer." A literal "not investment advice" sentence isn't in the data layer; that final consumer-facing disclaimer style comes from the gold-standard Shannon prototype the rendered report must match, so if legal wants the exact investment-advice wording, it's a one-line add to the writer guide.
