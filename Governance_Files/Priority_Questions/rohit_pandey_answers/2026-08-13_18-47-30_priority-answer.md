# Priority Question Answer

Question:
How does a dossier report ownership for leases with no 2025 County Appraisal District data?

Answer:
The dossier reports it honestly as unverified rather than papering over it: the ownership section states there is no 2025 CAD owner match for the lease ("no exact interest match"), the claimed interest is presented as claimed — falling back to the member's own claim-record interest, explicitly not title-verified (which also means the duplicate-inflation check and owner cross-check can't run for that lease). Everything else still populates from independent sources — production (RRC filings), MVestimate value, map and activity are unaffected. Per the standing rule these leases are informational, never framed as a bug or regression: they're appended to the running log Dossier - needs-data log (missing 2025 owner rows).md, not raised to Nikhil as fix requests, and the writer rules guarantee the prose never asserts ownership for them.
