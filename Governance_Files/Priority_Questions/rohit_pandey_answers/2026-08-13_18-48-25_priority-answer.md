# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Now that dossiers trust the member's verified claim as the ownership source, who verifies that claim is correct?

Answer:
The pipeline itself verifies every claim against the county appraisal roll — the claim is never trusted bare. owner_verify pulls all CAD owner records for the claimed lease and requires the claimed interest to match a recorded tranche (±5e-7), and name_matches fuzzy-compares the member's name to the recorded owner (nicknames, reordering, spelling) — the results drive the five review classifications, so only CAD-corroborated claims reach ✅ verified-final. A human (the dossier workstream owner) then reviews each dossier individually before release; mismatches route to relationship capture (Self/Spouse/Heir/Trustee) or the Needs Attention — Ownership Verification register, where the member must supply evidence. Legal title itself is verified by no one, by explicit policy — "we don't verify title or heirship; that's a records/attorney question" — the dossier only ever asserts CAD-recorded facts, never title.
