Why the historical period genuinely cannot yield a rate.

subscription_checkout_logs begins 5 Jun 2026 and holds 19 rows. Claims run from Nov 2025, and 113 of 192 claimers — 59% — first claimed before that date. No checkout-stage record exists for them.
subscriptionPayment has no date column — createTs is time without time zone, a clock time with no calendar date. Its 177 rows cannot be placed in a month or cohort, so no payment can be dated from the payments table and no lag computed from it.
checkoutpageinfo_api_track begins 28 Jul 2026.
braintree_payment_response has held nothing since Feb 2024 — no independent settlement confirmation.
userbehavior has no /pricing or /payment rows after 4 Aug 2026; spendingtime never populated reliably; 98% of click rows self-repeat. And sub_subscription_cancellations is QA-dominated, so it cannot serve as churn evidence.

Standing rule for historical figures: counts only, never a rate. Before 5 Jun 2026 the denominator is contaminated by synthetic accounts and the checkout stage was uninstrumented.
