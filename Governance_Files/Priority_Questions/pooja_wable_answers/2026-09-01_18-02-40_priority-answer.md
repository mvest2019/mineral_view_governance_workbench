Question:
Do share links expire or get revoked, and do the API numbers and lease identifiers they carry end up in server logs, referrers, or analytics?

Answer:
No expiry and no revocation — a share link is just query parameters on /map-explorer, with no token and nothing recorded server-side, so anyone holding it gets that view for as long as the app is up (subject to whatever sign-in the page itself requires).
