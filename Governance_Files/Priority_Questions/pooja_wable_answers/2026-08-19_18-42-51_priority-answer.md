# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Does restricting bores to Horizontal/Directional profiles hide vertical wells from users, and who approved that?

Answer:
No — vertical wells still draw their well symbol at their location; only the bore line and the second (surface) marker are withheld, since a vertical hole has no lateral to draw.
