# Priority Question Answer

Question:
What are the current baseline and target free-registration conversion numbers, and which system is the agreed source of truth?

Answer:
The baseline is the current rate at which site visitors complete a free registration, measured over the last full reporting period. The target is a step up from that baseline over the coming quarter, set as a percentage improvement rather than an absolute number, so it stays tied to traffic levels rather than assuming growth in visits.

Website traffic and funnel behaviour are tracked in Google Analytics, and completed registrations are confirmed against the product database. Where the two differ, the product database is treated as the source of truth for actual registration counts, with analytics used for the traffic and drop-off picture around it.

Both numbers are reviewed at the end of each reporting cycle alongside the rest of the marketing metrics, and the target is reset from there.
