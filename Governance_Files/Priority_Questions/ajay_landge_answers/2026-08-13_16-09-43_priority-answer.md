# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Which exact URLs are proposed for `noindex`, and who approves that list before it is applied?

Answer:
No URLs have been proposed for noindex yet. Krishna is working on this, but the list won't be compiled until after the marketing site development is complete and the new URL structure is fully stable. Building a noindex list against the current site while the redesign is in progress would mean working with URLs that may change, merge, or get removed entirely — so we're deliberately waiting.

Once the new site is live, Krishna will pull a fresh crawl and Search Console audit, filter for URLs meeting our noindex criteria — zero clicks, under 10 impressions over a 12-month window, no backlink equity, and no internal linking or conversion value — and compile the proposed list. That list then goes through a two-step approval process: the SEO lead signs off on the strategic rationale confirming no search value is being lost, and the dev lead signs off on the technical implementation confirming the noindex tag is scoped correctly and doesn't bleed into unintended pages through shared templates or layouts. For any bulk changes exceeding 10 or more URLs in a single batch, marketing or product leadership provides a final review as well.

Nothing gets applied in production without that sign-off chain complete and documented — every approved URL will have the supporting data, the approver name, and the approval date logged for a full audit trail.
