Question:
Which live pages will be noindexed, consolidated, or removed for crawl-budget reasons, and who approves that before it affects traffic?

Answer:
Nothing is being noindexed, consolidated or removed at this stage. The current SEO work is at the audit level — identifying thin, duplicate or low-value pages that dilute crawl budget and don't earn traffic or conversions.

Once that list exists, it comes back as a proposal with the reasoning per page, rather than being actioned directly. Anything that would affect a page currently earning traffic, holding backlinks, or forming part of the signup path stays live until it's explicitly approved.

Approval sits with whoever owns the site roadmap — decided between marketing and product, in coordination with the redesign team, since page consolidation overlaps directly with the redesign scope.
