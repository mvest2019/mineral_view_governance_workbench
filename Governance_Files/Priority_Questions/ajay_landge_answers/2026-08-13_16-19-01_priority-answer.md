# Priority Question Answer

Question:
How is access to the LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram accounts held, and is it under company-owned credentials?

Answer:
Yes, all social media accounts — LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram — are held under company-owned credentials. No account is tied to a personal email or an individual team member's personal login. Each account is registered and managed through company-owned email addresses, which ensures that access stays with the organization regardless of any team changes.

For day-to-day management, access is handled through each platform's built-in roles and permissions system. On Facebook and Instagram, accounts are managed through Meta Business Suite where team members are assigned specific roles — admin, editor, or analyst — based on what they need to do. LinkedIn follows the same approach with its company page admin roles. This way everyone has the access level they need to do their job without holding full ownership-level credentials.

Two-factor authentication is enabled on all accounts and tied to company-owned devices or authentication methods, not personal phone numbers. If a team member transitions off the project or leaves, their role is removed immediately and credentials are rotated as a standard precaution. There's an internal record of who currently has access to which platform and at what permission level, so there's never any ambiguity about who can post, edit, or manage account settings.

No third-party tool or app has been granted access to any of these accounts beyond what Meta and LinkedIn natively provide. Everything stays within the official platform ecosystems and under company control.
