Question:
Who approved the social media post before it was published, and on which account did it go live?

Answer:
Every social media post goes through internal review before it's published — nothing goes live without at least one approval from the marketing team. The process is straightforward: the content is drafted, reviewed for brand consistency, messaging accuracy, and platform-specific formatting, and then approved by the marketing lead before scheduling or posting. If a post includes product-specific claims — like anything about MVEstimate's capabilities or data accuracy — it gets an additional check to make sure the messaging matches what the product actually delivers.

Posts are published across the relevant client channel accounts managed through Trendelier. For Mineral View specifically, content goes live on the Mineral View Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook accounts depending on the content type and target audience. Reel content promoting MVEstimate is pushed across all three platforms with format adjustments for each — vertical short-form for Instagram and TikTok, professional framing for LinkedIn, and broader engagement-friendly formatting for Facebook.

Each published post is logged with the date it went live, which platform and account it was posted to, who drafted it, and who gave the final approval. That way if there's ever a question about a specific post — why it was published, what it says, or who signed off — we can trace it back immediately without guessing. If any post needs to be pulled or edited after going live, the same approval chain applies before changes are made.
