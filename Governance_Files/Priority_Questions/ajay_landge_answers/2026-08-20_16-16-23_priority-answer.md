# Priority Question Answer

Question:
How do these website conversion changes coordinate with the in-flight marketing site and portal redesign work, and who resolves conflicts?

Answer:
The conversion-focused changes are smaller, iterative updates to existing pages — copy, calls to action and signup flow messaging — aimed at improving how visitors move from the site into the product. The marketing site and portal redesign is a larger, separate workstream running in parallel.

To keep the two aligned, any conversion change touching a page inside the redesign scope is flagged before it goes live, so the same page isn't being edited in two places. Where a change would simply be overwritten by the redesign, it's either held until after launch or passed to the redesign team to build in directly. Changes on pages outside the redesign scope go ahead as normal.

Where the two overlap and a decision is needed, it goes to whoever owns the site roadmap — currently resolved between marketing and the product team, with the redesign taking precedence on shared pages.
