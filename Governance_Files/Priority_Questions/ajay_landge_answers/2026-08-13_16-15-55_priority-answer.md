# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Is "Trendelier" a Mineral View brand, a separate product, or an external client?

Answer:
Trendelier is the product itself — it's our content and social-scanning platform that handles channels, scans, insights, content drafts, and the analytics dashboard. It's not a Mineral View brand or a separate external client. It's the other way around — Mineral View and BOLD are client channels and brands that are managed inside Trendelier. Think of Trendelier as the engine and the individual brands as the accounts running through it. The platform, the codebase, and the product roadmap all live under Trendelier. Any brand-specific work we do, whether it's social content, SEO, or profile updates, is executed through the Trendelier platform for whichever client channel it belongs to.
