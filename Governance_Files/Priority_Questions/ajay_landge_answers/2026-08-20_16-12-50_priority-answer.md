Question:
Who holds the login credentials for the Mineral View LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram accounts, and how is access revoked?

Answer:
Access to the Mineral View LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram accounts sits with the marketing team. I have day-to-day access for posting and monitoring across all three.

Instagram and Facebook are managed through Meta Business Suite, and LinkedIn through the company page admin settings — both grant access per user rather than through a shared login.

Revoking access: when someone leaves the team or changes roles, marketing removes their user permission from Meta Business Suite and from the LinkedIn page admin list. Since access is permission-based, removing the user is enough — no credential reset is needed.
