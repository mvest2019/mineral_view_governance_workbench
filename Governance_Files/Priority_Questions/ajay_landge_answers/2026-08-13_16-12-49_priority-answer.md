# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Who approved the branding and profile information updates before they went live?

Answer:
All branding and profile information updates go through internal review and approval before anything goes live. The marketing team handles the initial preparation — ensuring logos, brand colors, taglines, descriptions, and any visual assets are consistent with Mineral View's brand guidelines across all platforms and profiles.

Before publishing, the updates are reviewed by the marketing lead for brand consistency and messaging accuracy, making sure everything aligns with how we want Mineral View and MVEstimate positioned in the market. If the update touches anything product-related — like feature descriptions, capability claims, or tool-specific language — the product team reviews as well to confirm what's being said matches what the platform actually does. We don't put out messaging that overpromises or misrepresents functionality.

For platform-specific profiles — whether that's Google Business, social media bios, directory listings, or any third-party profile — the same review applies. Any changes to company name, description, contact details, or links are verified for accuracy before going live. If a branding update is part of a broader effort like the marketing site redesign, it gets bundled into that project's review cycle so everything launches in sync rather than piecemeal.

No branding or profile change goes live without at least one approval from the marketing lead, and for anything customer-facing that involves product claims, the product side signs off as well.
