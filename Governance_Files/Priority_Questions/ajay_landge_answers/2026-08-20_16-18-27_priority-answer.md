# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Do the proposed registration-conversion improvements remove or weaken email verification, consent, or any required signup step?

Answer:
No. The conversion work is limited to how the signup is presented and communicated — clearer copy, better placement of calls to action, reducing unnecessary friction in the flow, and making the value of registering more obvious before the form.

Email verification, consent capture and any legally or functionally required signup steps stay exactly as they are. Nothing in the proposed changes removes a verification step, pre-ticks or bypasses a consent checkbox, or defers a required field to a later stage. The aim is to get more of the right visitors to complete the existing flow, not to shorten the flow by dropping controls.

Any change that would touch verification or consent would go to legal and product before it was proposed, not after.
