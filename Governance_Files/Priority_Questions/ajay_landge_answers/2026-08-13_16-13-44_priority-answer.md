# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Which social accounts and platforms were changed, and who holds the credentials?

Answer:
The social accounts we actively manage and post content through for Mineral View are Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok. These are the platforms where we're publishing reel content and promotional material for MVEstimate and broader Mineral View brand awareness. Any updates to profile information, bios, links, or branding on these accounts go through the internal review process before being applied.

As for credentials, access is managed by the marketing team with credentials held securely by designated team members only. No login details are shared loosely or stored in open documents. Each platform account has a primary owner who holds admin-level access, and any additional team members who need posting or editing access are added through the platform's built-in roles and permissions — so they get the access level they need without holding full admin credentials.

If any account credentials need to be updated, rotated, or if a team member's access needs to be revoked, the primary account owner handles that directly. We also ensure two-factor authentication is enabled on all accounts to prevent unauthorized access. A record of who has access to which platform is maintained internally so there's no ambiguity about who can do what. If a team member leaves or transitions off the project, their access is removed immediately and credentials are rotated as a standard practice.
