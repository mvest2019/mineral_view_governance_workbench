# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Which exact URLs are approved for `noindex`, and who signs off before it is applied in production?

Answer:
The exact list of URLs approved for noindex is maintained in our internal SEO audit sheet, where each URL is flagged with its current indexing status, impression and click data from Search Console, and the reason for the noindex recommendation — whether that's thin content, duplicate, parameter bloat, or zero search value over the review window.

As for sign-off, no noindex tag goes into production without approval from both the SEO lead and the dev/engineering lead. The SEO lead confirms the page has no strategic value worth preserving — no meaningful impressions, no backlinks worth keeping, no keyword coverage gap it would create. The dev lead confirms the implementation is correct, meaning the meta tag or header is applied only to the intended URLs and doesn't accidentally cascade to other pages through a template or layout component. For bulk noindex changes — say anything over 10 URLs at once — we also loop in the product or marketing lead for a final check, since removing pages from the index at scale can affect brand visibility and internal linking structure. Everything is documented with the date, the approver, and the rationale so we have a clear audit trail if anything needs to be reversed.
