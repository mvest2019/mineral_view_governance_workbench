# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Who owns the Trendelier brand, and is it a Mineral View property or a separate client?

Answer:
Trendelier is not a Mineral View property — it's the other way around. Trendelier is the platform and the product. It's our content and social-scanning platform that powers everything — channels, scans, insights, content drafts, and the analytics dashboard. It operates as its own product with its own codebase and roadmap.

Mineral View is one of the client brands managed inside Trendelier, alongside other brands like BOLD. Think of Trendelier as the central platform and the brands as accounts running through it. Trendelier owns the infrastructure, the tooling, and the workflows. The client brands use Trendelier's capabilities to manage their social presence, content strategy, and analytics.

So to be clear on the hierarchy — Trendelier is the product company, and Mineral View is a client channel within that product. Trendelier is not a sub-brand, feature, or property of Mineral View. The ownership, the brand, and the product identity all sit with Trendelier independently.
