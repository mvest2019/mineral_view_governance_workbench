Question:
Does this crawl-budget plan account for the in-flight marketing site redesign that may change these same URLs?

Answer:
Yes, the crawl-budget plan takes the in-flight marketing site redesign into consideration. We're aware that the redesign may introduce new URL structures, merge or retire existing pages, and update templates — all of which directly impact crawl behavior and indexing. That's exactly why we're being conservative with any noindex or redirect changes right now and only acting on pages that are clearly dead weight regardless of what the redesign does.

For anything that overlaps with the redesign scope, we're holding off and coordinating with the design and dev team so we don't end up doing double work — applying a noindex today only to have the page restructured or removed entirely next month. The plan is to lock in the low-risk cleanup now, like parameter pages, obvious duplicates, and zero-traffic clutter, and then revisit the full crawl-budget strategy once the new site architecture is finalized. At that point we'll do a fresh crawl audit against the new URL map, update the sitemap, and set up proper redirects for any legacy URLs that change. The goal is to make sure nothing we do today conflicts with or has to be undone when the redesign goes live.
