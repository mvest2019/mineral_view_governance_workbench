# Priority Question Answer

Question:
Does the user-segment sales and marketing work use real member records or PII, and is that data access approved?

Answer:
The segmentation work is based on aggregated and anonymised data — behavioural and usage patterns, portfolio size bands, activity levels — rather than individual member records. No personally identifiable information is exported, copied into marketing tools, or used to build creative or campaign material.

Where segment definitions need to be applied to actual users, that happens inside the product systems by the team that already holds approved access, rather than by pulling member records out into marketing.

Any work that would require direct access to member PII would go through approval with product and legal before it started.
