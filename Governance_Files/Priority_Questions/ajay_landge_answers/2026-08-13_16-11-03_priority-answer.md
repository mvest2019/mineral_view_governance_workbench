Question:
Where is the content-opportunity research and the testing/validation evidence stored?

Answer:
The content-opportunity research is stored in our internal SEO documentation — this includes keyword research, competitor gap analysis, search intent mapping, and topic cluster planning. Krishna is maintaining this alongside the broader SEO audit work. The primary data sources feeding into it are Google Search Console for impression and click data, and any keyword research tools we're using to identify gaps and opportunities across our target topics like mineral rights, valuations, and oil and gas terminology.

For testing and validation evidence, everything is tracked in the same SEO tracking sheet where we log our baselines. This covers before-and-after metrics for any changes we make — page indexing status snapshots, performance data like impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position, plus crawl reports showing how Google is handling our pages. The August 13, 2026 Search Console snapshot with the 30,196 "Crawled – currently not indexed" count is one example of documented validation evidence already in place.

Once the marketing site redesign is complete and Krishna begins executing on the noindex and content improvement work, each action will have its own validation entry — what was changed, when it was applied, what the metrics looked like before and after, and whether the change achieved the intended result. That way if anyone asks why a decision was made or whether it worked, the evidence is documented and easy to trace back.
